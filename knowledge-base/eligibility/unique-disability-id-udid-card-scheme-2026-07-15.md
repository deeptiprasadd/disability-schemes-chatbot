## Overview
The Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card Scheme is an initiative by the Government of India to provide a unique identity card to persons with disabilities. This card aims to simplify the process of availing benefits and schemes provided by the government for people with disabilities. The UDID card serves as a single document for verification of disability, reducing the need for multiple documents.

## Benefits
- Simplified process for availing government benefits and schemes
- Single document for verification of disability
- Easy access to information and services for persons with disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- The applicant must be a citizen of India
- The applicant must have a disability as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability (medical certificate)
2. Proof of age (birth certificate, Aadhaar card, etc.)
3. Proof of income (income certificate, etc.)
4. Proof of residence (Aadhaar card, etc.)

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the official website of the UDID Card Scheme (https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in) and fill the online application form.
Step 2: Upload the required documents and submit the application.
Step 3: Take a printout of the application and visit the nearest Common Service Center (CSC) or District Disability Rehabilitation Center (DDRC) for verification.
Step 4: After verification, the UDID card will be issued.
