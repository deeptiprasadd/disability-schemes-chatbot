## Overview
The Admission to BPT & BOT Programmes Through NEET(UG) is an initiative by the National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Multiple Disabilities (NIEPMD) to provide admission to Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) and Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (BOT) programs through the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET). 

## Benefits
- Admission to BPT and BOT programs for persons with disabilities
- Opportunity to pursue a career in physiotherapy and occupational therapy

## Eligibility criteria
- Individuals with disabilities as enumerated in the RPWD Act (2016)
- Qualifying NEET(UG) exam

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability
2. NEET(UG) exam scorecard
3. Proof of age and identity
