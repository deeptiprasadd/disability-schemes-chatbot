## Overview
The Common Entrance Test (CET -2026) is an entrance exam conducted by the National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Multiple Disabilities (NIEPMD) for admission to Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT), Bachelor of Occupational Therapy (BOT), and Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP) programs. 

## Benefits
- Admission to BPT, BOT, and BASLP programs for persons with disabilities
- Opportunity to pursue a career in physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and audiology and speech-language pathology

## Eligibility criteria
- Individuals with disabilities as enumerated in the RPWD Act (2016)
- Qualifying CET -2026 exam

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability
2. CET -2026 exam scorecard
3. Proof of age and identity
