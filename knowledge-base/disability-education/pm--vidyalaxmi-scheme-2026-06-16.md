## Overview
The PM- VIDYALAXMI SCHEME is a government initiative aimed at providing educational support to persons with disabilities. The scheme is part of the National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Multiple Disabilities (NIEPMD) and is designed to empower individuals with multiple disabilities. 

## Benefits
- Educational support for persons with disabilities
- Empowerment of individuals with multiple disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Individuals with multiple disabilities
- Individuals with disabilities as enumerated in the RPWD Act (2016)

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability
2. Proof of age and identity

Step by step instructions are not provided, but applicants can visit the NIEPMD website or contact the institute for more information.
