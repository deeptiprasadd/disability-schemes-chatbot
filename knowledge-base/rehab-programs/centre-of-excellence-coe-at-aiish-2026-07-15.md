## Overview
The Centre of Excellence (CoE) at AIISH is a facility dedicated to advancing research, clinical services, and education in the field of communication disorders. The CoE was dedicated to the nation by the Prime Minister of India in 2022.

## Benefits
- Advanced research facilities
- Specialized clinical services
- Education and training opportunities

## Eligibility criteria
- Individuals with communication disorders
- Researchers and clinicians in the field of communication disorders

## Required documents
1. Application form
2. Documents showcasing research or clinical work in communication disorders
