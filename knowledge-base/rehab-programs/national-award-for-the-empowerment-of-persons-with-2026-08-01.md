## Overview
The National Award for the Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities is a prestigious award given to institutions that have made significant contributions to the empowerment of persons with disabilities. The All India Institute of Speech and Hearing (AIISH) received this award in 2024. The award recognizes the institution's efforts in providing education, research, and clinical services to persons with communication disorders.

## Benefits
- Recognition of the institution's efforts in empowering persons with disabilities
- Encouragement to continue providing high-quality services to persons with communication disorders
- Opportunity to share best practices and expertise with other institutions

## Eligibility criteria
- The institution must have made significant contributions to the empowerment of persons with disabilities
- The institution must have a strong track record of providing education, research, and clinical services to persons with communication disorders

## Required documents
1. Application form
2. Documents showcasing the institution's contributions to the empowerment of persons with disabilities
