## Overview
The National Award for the Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities is an award given to recognize the efforts of institutions and individuals in empowering persons with disabilities. AIISH received this award in 2024. The award aims to promote the rights and inclusion of persons with disabilities in society.

## Benefits
- Recognition of efforts in empowering persons with disabilities
- Promotion of inclusion and rights of persons with disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Institutions and individuals working towards empowering persons with disabilities

## Required documents
1. Application form
2. Documents showcasing work and efforts in empowering persons with disabilities
