## Overview
SAMBHAV sets up resource centres in cities with populations over 5 million (2011 census) to collect, display and demonstrate assistive aids, devices and software for persons with disabilities.

## Benefits
- Access to information and demonstration of assistive devices.  
- Maintenance of a database of aids on the National Trust website.

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities covered under the National Trust Act seeking information or demonstration of assistive devices.
