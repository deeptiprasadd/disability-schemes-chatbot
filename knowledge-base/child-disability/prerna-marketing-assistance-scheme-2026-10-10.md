## Overview
PRERNA provides marketing assistance to Registered Organisations (ROs) to promote products and services made by persons with the four National Trust disabilities, funding participation in fairs, exhibitions and other events.

## Benefits
- Funds for RO participation in national, regional, state and district level events.  
- Incentive to RO based on sales turnover of products made by PwDs; at least 51 % of employees in work‑centres must be PwDs.

## Eligibility criteria
- Registered Organisations under the National Trust.  
- Products/services must be produced by PwDs with the four covered disabilities; ≥ 51 % of work‑centre staff must be PwDs.
