## Overview
The Cross-Disability Early Intervention Centres (CDEIC) provide specialized early childhood intervention services for children aged 0‑6 years who are at risk, have a disability, or experience developmental delay. These centres aim to support the child’s development, well‑being, and participation in family and community life, enabling an independent and dignified future.
