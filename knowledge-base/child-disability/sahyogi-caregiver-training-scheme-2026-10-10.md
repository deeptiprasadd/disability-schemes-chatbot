## Overview
SAHYOGI establishes Caregiver Cells to train a skilled workforce of caregivers for persons with disabilities and their families, offering primary and advanced level courses.

## Benefits
- Training programmes for caregivers (primary and advanced levels).  
- Creation of a skilled caregiver workforce for NGOs, work‑centres and families.

## Eligibility criteria
- Individuals (including parents) who wish to receive caregiver training.  
- No restriction on specific disability type (covers all).
