## Overview
Cross-Disability Early Intervention Centres (CDEICs) provide specialized early childhood intervention services for children (0–6 years) with disabilities or developmental delays and support their families. The centres aim to promote development, well‑being, and participation of children in family and community life during a critical period of brain development.
