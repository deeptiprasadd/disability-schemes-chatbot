## Overview
GHARAUNDA offers assured lifelong home and minimum quality care services for adults with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation and Multiple Disabilities, including vocational and pre‑vocational activities.

## Benefits
- Continuous residential care with acceptable living standards and basic medical care.  
- Provision of vocational and pre‑vocational activities and assistance for further training.

## Eligibility criteria
- Adults with any of the four disabilities covered under the National Trust Act.
