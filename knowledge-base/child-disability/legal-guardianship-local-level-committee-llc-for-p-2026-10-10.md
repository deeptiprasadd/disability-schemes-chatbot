## Overview
This scheme provides a framework for appointing legal guardians for persons with disabilities through the National Trust’s Local Level Committees (LLCs). While guardianship is not mandatory, it is advantageous for individuals who cannot manage personal, financial, or property matters on their own.

## Benefits
- Legal representation of the person with disability in personal and property matters.  
- Protection of the ward’s interests and assets through a formally appointed guardian.  
- Ability to appoint separate guardians for personal care and property management if needed.

## Eligibility criteria
- The guardian must be a citizen of India (Section 17(1)(iii)(a) NT Rules).  
- Both guardian and ward must reside within the territorial jurisdiction of the concerned LLC (Regulation 12(6)).  
- The guardian cannot be a minor (must be 18 years or older).  
- A “friend” cannot apply directly; a parent, sibling, or relative must recommend or apply.  
- Registered organizations (societies, trusts, associations) may act as LLCs and apply for guardianship on behalf of a ward.  
- The ward must belong to the area specified for the LLC (Section 13(1) NT Act).

## How to apply
1. Identify the Local Level Committee (LLC) that has jurisdiction over the ward’s residence.  
2. Submit an application for guardianship to the LLC (or have a registered organization submit on behalf of the ward).  
3. Provide details of the proposed guardian, including proof of Indian citizenship and residence within the LLC’s jurisdiction.  
4. The LLC may request security (guarantee, undertaking, or bond) from the proposed guardian.  
5. The LLC will review the application, may call for evidence or witnesses, and issue a decision.  
6. If the guardian is later convicted, incapacitated, bankrupt, or otherwise unable to serve, the LLC will remove the guardian and appoint a new one.
