## Overview
BADHTE KADAM supports Registered Organisations to conduct awareness, sensitisation, social integration and mainstreaming activities for National Trust disabilities.

## Benefits
- Sponsorship of up to 4 events per RO per year.  
- Requirement that each RO conducts at least one community, educational or medical institute event annually.

## Eligibility criteria
- Registered Organisations under the National Trust seeking to run awareness/community events.
