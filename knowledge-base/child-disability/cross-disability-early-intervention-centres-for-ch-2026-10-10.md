## Overview
Cross-Disability Early Intervention Centres (CDEICs) provide specialized early childhood intervention services for children aged 0–6 years who have disabilities or developmental delays, along with support for their families. The centres aim to promote development, well‑being, and participation in family and community life during a critical period of brain development.
