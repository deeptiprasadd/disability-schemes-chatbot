## Overview
SAMARTH provides respite homes for orphans, abandoned children, families in crisis, and persons with disabilities from BPL & LIG families (including destitutes) who have at least one of the four National Trust disabilities.

## Benefits
- Group‑home facility for all age groups with quality care and acceptable living standards.  
- Provision of basic medical care from professional doctors.

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with at least one of the four disabilities covered under the National Trust Act.  
- Belonging to BPL or LIG families, or destitute/orphaned status.
