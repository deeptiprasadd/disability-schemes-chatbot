## Overview
Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK) is a national programme aimed at early identification and early intervention for children from birth to 18 years. It covers the “4 Ds”: Defects at birth, Deficiencies, Diseases, and Development delays including disability, providing free screening, diagnosis and treatment through a network of health facilities and mobile health teams.

## Benefits
- Free screening of 30 selected health conditions (including birth defects, deficiencies, diseases, developmental delays, adolescent health issues).  
- Free treatment/intervention for identified conditions, delivered at no cost to the child and family.  
- Referral linkage through District Early Intervention Centres (DEIC) for comprehensive care.  
- Mobile health teams provide outreach screening at Anganwadi Centres (6 weeks‑6 years) and schools (6‑18 years).  

## Eligibility criteria
- Children aged **0‑6 years** (rural areas and those born in public health facilities).  
- Children aged **6‑18 years** enrolled in classes I‑XII in government and government‑aided schools.  
- All listed health conditions (defects at birth, deficiencies, diseases of childhood, developmental delays/disabilities, adolescent health concerns) are covered.

## How to apply
1. **Screening at birth** – Medical Officers, Staff Nurses and ANMs screen newborns at delivery points.  
2. **Home screening (0‑6 weeks)** – ASHA workers conduct screening at home as part of the HBNC package.  
3. **Outreach screening (6 weeks‑6 years)** – Mobile health teams visit Anganwadi Centres for preschool children.  
4. **School screening (6‑18 years)** – Mobile health teams visit schools for children enrolled in classes I‑XII.  
5. If a child is identified with any of the 30 conditions, the team refers the child to the nearest District Early Intervention Centre (DEIC) or appropriate public health facility.  
6. Treatment/intervention is provided free of cost at the referred facility.
