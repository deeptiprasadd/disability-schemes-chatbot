## Overview
Samagra Shiksha in Chandigarh aims to provide free and compulsory education up to age 18 for all children, including those with special needs (CWSN). The scheme integrates CWSN into mainstream schools, offers home‑based education where needed, and provides a range of support services to ensure inclusive, quality education.

## Benefits
- Curriculum modification and development of Individualized Education Plans (IEP)  
- Evaluation guidelines for CWSN  
- Capacity building of general teachers  
- Learning corners for CWSN  
- Support services in Special Resource Centres  
- Therapy camps at cluster level  
- Training on sports and skills  
- Open schooling for children with intellectual disabilities at secondary level  
- Transport & escort allowance  
- Stipend to girl CWSN  
- Medical assessment and provision of aids & appliances  
- Home Based Education (HBE) for eligible children  

## Eligibility criteria
- Child must be identified as a Child with Special Needs (CWSN) by the Regional Teams (RTs).  
- Medical examination by the medical teams of Govt. Medical College & Hospitals, Sector‑32 (GMCH‑32) or Govt. Multispecialty Hospital, Sector‑16 (GMSH‑16) under the Directorate of Health, UT Chandigarh to ascertain degree and type of disability.  
- Enrollment either in mainstream schools (U‑DISE) or through Home Based Education as per the numbers listed (e.g., 4,087 enrolled in schools, 217 covered through HBE).

## How to apply
1. **Identification** – Regional Teams (RTs) identify the child as CWSN.  
2. **Medical verification** – Obtain a medical examination from GMCH‑32 or GMSH‑16 to confirm the disability type and degree.  
3. **Enrollment** – Register the child in a mainstream school (U‑DISE) or apply for Home Based Education if appropriate.  
4. **Submit documentation** – Provide the medical certificate and any school‑level forms required for enrollment or HBE placement.  
5. **Access benefits** – Once enrolled, the child will receive the applicable support services, allowances, and aids as listed under Benefits.
