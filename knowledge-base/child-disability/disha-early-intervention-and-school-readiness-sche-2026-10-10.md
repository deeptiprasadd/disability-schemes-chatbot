## Overview
DISHA is an early‑intervention and school‑readiness scheme for children aged 0‑10 years who have any of the four disabilities covered under the National Trust Act. It aims to set up Disha Centres that provide therapies, trainings and family support.

## Benefits
- Day‑care facilities for at least 4 hours a day (8 am – 6 pm) with age‑specific activities.  
- Access to a Special Educator or Early Intervention Therapist, Physiotherapist or Occupational Therapist, and a Counsellor, plus caregiver and Ayas at the centre.

## Eligibility criteria
- Child aged 0‑10 years.  
- Must have one of the four disabilities covered under the National Trust Act (Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation, Multiple Disabilities).
