## Overview
This scheme outlines the powers and functions of a Local Level Committee (LLC) under the National Trust Regulations for persons with disability. It details the LLC’s authority to assess needs, appoint guardians, handle property disputes, and report criminal offences.

## Benefits
- Authority to request the production of a person with disability for needs assessment.  
- Power to appoint guardians for property management, even outside the LLC’s jurisdiction.  
- Ability to direct parties in property disputes to resolve issues through a civil court.  
- Right to report criminal offences by guardians to the Area Magistrate.

## Eligibility criteria
- The matters must involve a person with disability as defined under the National Trust Regulations.  
- The LLC must be acting within its statutory powers as per Sections 13(2) and 13(5).

## How to apply
1. Identify the need for assessment or guardian appointment concerning a person with disability.  
2. Contact the relevant Local Level Committee.  
3. Provide necessary details of the person with disability and the property or issue in question.  
4. The LLC will issue directions for production, appointment, or dispute resolution as per the regulations.  
5. If criminal activity is suspected, the LLC may file a complaint with the Area Magistrate or advise the aggrieved person to do so.
