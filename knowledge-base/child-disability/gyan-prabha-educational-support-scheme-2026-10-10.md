## Overview
GYAN PRABHA encourages persons with the four National Trust disabilities to pursue educational or vocational courses, providing financial assistance for fees and related expenses.

## Benefits
- Specific amount per course to cover fees, transportation, books, and out‑of‑pocket expenses.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation or Multiple Disabilities seeking higher education or vocational training.
