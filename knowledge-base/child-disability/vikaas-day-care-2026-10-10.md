## Overview
VIKAAS is a day‑care scheme that expands opportunities for persons with disability to develop interpersonal and vocational skills as they transition to higher age groups, while also providing caregiving support to families.

## Benefits
- Day‑care facilities for at least 6 hours a day (8 am – 6 pm) with age‑specific activities.  
- Centre must operate at least 21 days a month and provide caregiving support to the PwD.

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with any of the four disabilities covered under the National Trust Act.  
- No specific age limit mentioned (implied to be beyond early‑childhood).
