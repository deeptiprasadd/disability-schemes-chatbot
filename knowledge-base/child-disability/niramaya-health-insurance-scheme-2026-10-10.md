## Overview
NIRAMAYA provides affordable health‑insurance coverage to persons with the four National Trust disabilities, offering up to Rs 1.0 lakh per beneficiary.

## Benefits
- Health‑insurance cover up to Rs 1.0 lakh covering OPD treatment, medicines, pathology, diagnostics, regular medical check‑up, dental care, preventive surgeries, hospitalization, corrective surgeries, ongoing therapies, alternative medicine and transportation costs.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation or Multiple Disabilities.
