## Overview
The National Fund for Persons with Disabilities is a scheme established by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities. The scheme aims to promote the empowerment of persons with disabilities and ensure their full participation in society.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for persons with disabilities
- Promotion of empowerment of persons with disabilities
- Full participation in society

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016
- Indian citizens

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability
2. Proof of age
3. Proof of income
4. Proof of residence

## How to apply
Step by step instructions are not provided in the given text. However, persons with disabilities can contact the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) or the National Commission for Persons with Disabilities for more information on how to apply for benefits under the scheme.
