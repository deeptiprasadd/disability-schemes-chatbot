## Overview
The National Fund for Persons with Disabilities is a scheme that provides financial assistance to persons with disabilities for their empowerment and development. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD).

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for education, employment, and healthcare
- Assistance for assistive devices and accessibility features
- Support for self-employment and entrepreneurship

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016
- Indian citizens
- Persons with disabilities who require financial assistance

## Required documents
1. Disability certificate
2. Identity proof
3. Address proof
4. Income certificate

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the official website of the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)
Step 2: Fill out the online application form
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Submit the application
