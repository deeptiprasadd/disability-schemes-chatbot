## Overview
The ADIP Scheme, operational since 1981, aims to assist needy persons with disabilities in procuring modern, certified assistive devices and appliances that promote physical, social and psychological rehabilitation, and enhance economic potential. The scheme also provides for corrective surgeries when required before the provision of an assistive device.

## Benefits
- Provision of durable, scientifically manufactured aids and appliances (e.g., prostheses, wheelchairs, hearing aids).  
- Conduct of corrective surgeries, wherever required, prior to device provision.  
- Grants‑in‑aid released to implementing agencies (ALIMCO, National Institutes, Regional Centres, District Rehabilitation Centres, State Corporations, NGOs) for purchase and distribution of devices.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities who are **needy** and require assistive devices.  
- Devices must have due certification.  
- Beneficiaries must possess an Aadhaar Card (required for both adults and children as per Gazette notifications dated 29‑02‑2024).  

## Required documents
1. Aadhaar Card (adult or child).  
2. Medical certification confirming disability and need for the specific assistive device.  
3. Any additional documents as specified in the scheme’s detailed guidelines (not stated in the source).  

## How to apply
1. Visit the **Arjun Portal** (link provided in the source).  
2. Register / log in with your credentials.  
3. Fill in the application form for an assistive device, attaching required documents (Aadhaar, medical certificate, etc.).  
4. Submit the application; the portal will generate a reference number.  
5. Await verification and approval by the implementing agency.
