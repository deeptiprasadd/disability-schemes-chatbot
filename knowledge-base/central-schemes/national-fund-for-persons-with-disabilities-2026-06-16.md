## Overview
The National Fund for Persons with Disabilities is a fund established by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) to provide financial assistance to persons with disabilities.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for persons with disabilities
- Assistance for education, employment, and rehabilitation

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities are eligible for the scheme
- Specific eligibility criteria may vary depending on the type of assistance

## Required documents
1. Disability certificate
2. ID proof
3. Address proof
4. Income certificate

## How to apply
Step by step instructions to apply for the scheme:
1. Visit the official website of the DEPwD
2. Fill the online application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit the application
5. Track the application status
