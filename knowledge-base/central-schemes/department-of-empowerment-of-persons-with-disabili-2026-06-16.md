## Overview
The Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) is a department under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment that aims to empower persons with disabilities. The department provides various schemes and services to persons with disabilities, including disability certificates, UDID cards, and other benefits.

## Benefits
- Disability certificates and UDID cards
- Various schemes and services for persons with disabilities
- Employment opportunities for persons with disabilities
- Accessibility features such as video relay services and divya voicebot

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities are eligible for the schemes and services provided by the DEPwD
- Specific eligibility criteria may vary depending on the scheme or service

## Required documents
1. Disability certificate
2. ID proof
3. Address proof

## How to apply
Step by step instructions to apply for disability certificate and UDID card:
1. Visit the official website of the DEPwD
2. Fill the online application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit the application
5. Track the application status
