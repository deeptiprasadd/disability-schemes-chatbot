## Overview
The National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD) is an autonomous organization under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD). The institute aims to promote the empowerment of persons with visual disabilities and provide them with education, training, and employment opportunities.

## Benefits
- Education and training for persons with visual disabilities
- Employment opportunities for persons with visual disabilities
- Promotion of empowerment of persons with visual disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with visual disabilities as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016
- Indian citizens

## Required documents
1. Proof of visual disability
2. Proof of age
3. Proof of income
4. Proof of residence

## How to apply
Step by step instructions are not provided in the given text. However, persons with visual disabilities can contact the National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities (NIEPVD) for more information on how to apply for benefits under the scheme.
