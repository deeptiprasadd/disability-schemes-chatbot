## Overview
The ADIP Scheme provides assistive devices, kits, and suitable learning materials to persons with disabilities, including multiple disabilities, leprosy‑cured persons, and newly recognized disabilities under the RPWD Act 2016. The scheme is administered by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan) and aims to ensure timely distribution of aids through camps, headquarters, a mobile app, and an online portal.

## Benefits
- Provision of any suitable device/kit/learning material as recommended by the Expert Committee.  
- Assistive Daily Living (ADL) kits for leprosy‑cured persons.  
- Periodic revision of the list of assistive devices by the Department.  
- Administrative allowance of up to 5 % of grant‑in‑aid for awareness, assessment, and distribution activities (additional 5 % for mega camps).  

## Eligibility criteria
- Beneficiaries must be persons with disabilities (including multiple disabilities, leprosy‑cured persons, and new disabilities added in RPWD 2016).  
- Implementation agencies must obtain a certificate of the beneficiary’s monthly income from the competent authority.  
- Beneficiaries must not have received similar aid from any other source in the last three years (one year for children below 12 years).  
- Implementation agencies must upload beneficiary data on the MIS portal within two days of distribution.  

## Required documents
1. Copy of Registration Certificate under **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** (u/s 51/52).  
2. Copy of Registration Certificate under **Societies Registration Act, 1860** or **Charitable Trust Act** (as applicable).  
3. Names and details of the Management Committee members.  
4. PAN and Aadhaar details of trustees/members.  
5. Copy of the organization’s Rules, Aims and Objectives.  
6. Certified audited accounts and annual report for the previous year (or last three years for first‑time applicants).  
7. Utilization Certificate as per GFR 2017.  
8. Undertaking from the beneficiary confirming non‑receipt of similar aid from other sources.  

## How to apply
1. **Prepare the required documents** listed above.  
2. **Submit the application** directly to the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
3. For NGOs/Voluntary Organizations, **upload the proposal on the e‑Anudaan portal** (www.grants‑msje.gov.in).  
4. The proposal will be reviewed by the Screening Committee; physical inspection may be required for new NGOs/VOs.  
5. Upon approval, the Department will sanction financial assistance and issue a Utilization Certificate.  
6. Implementing agencies must **distribute aids within six months** of registration (via camps, headquarters, mobile app, or MIS portal) and upload beneficiary data as prescribed.
