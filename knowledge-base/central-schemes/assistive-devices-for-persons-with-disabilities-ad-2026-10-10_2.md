## Overview
The Assistive Devices for Persons with Disabilities (ADIP) Scheme is a centrally‑funded programme implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India. It aims to provide approved aids and devices to persons with disabilities through implementing agencies, with special emphasis on women and socially disadvantaged groups.

## Benefits
- Provision of approved aids and devices (e.g., wheelchairs, tricycles, prostheses) to eligible beneficiaries.  
- Financial assistance to implementing agencies for procurement and distribution of these aids, subject to cost‑ceiling norms.
