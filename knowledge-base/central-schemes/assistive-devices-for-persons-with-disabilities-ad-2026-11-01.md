## Overview
The Assistive Devices for Persons with Disabilities (ADIP) Scheme provides financial assistance for the procurement of aids and appliances to persons with disabilities (PwDs) across India. The scheme covers a wide range of devices, from low‑cost aids to high‑end prostheses and cochlear implants, with assistance levels based on income and disability severity.

## Benefits
- Full financial assistance for aids/appliances costing up to **Rs. 15,000**.  
- Partial assistance (up to **Rs. 15,000**) for items costing between **Rs. 15,001 – Rs. 30,000**.  
- 50 % cost sharing for expensive items above **Rs. 30,001** (except cochlear implants and motorized tricycles), subject to income ceiling and a 20 % budget limit.  
- **Cochlear implant** coverage up to **Rs. 7 lakh** (pre‑lingual, 1‑5 yr) or **Rs. 6 lakh** (acquired, 5‑18 yr), including surgery, therapy, mapping, travel and pre‑implant assessment.  
- Travel allowance up to **Rs. 250** per person (beneficiary + one escort) for camp visits.  
- Boarding & lodging allowance **Rs. 100 per day** for up to 15 days for eligible beneficiaries (income ≤ Rs. 22,500 / month).  
- Specific subsidies: high‑end prostheses (Rs. 30,000), motorized tricycles/wheelchairs (Rs. 50,000) for severe locomotor disability (≥ 80 %); assistive devices for visual, hearing, intellectual and developmental disabilities as listed.

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian citizen of any age.  
- Possession of a **UDID card** (or enrollment number) and a **Disability Certificate** showing **≥ 40 %** disability (temporary certificates accepted for children ≤ 6 years for TLM kits).  
- Valid **Aadhaar** number or enrollment ID.  
- Monthly family income **≤ Rs. 30,000** (or ≤ Rs. 22,500 for boarding/lodging allowance).  
- No receipt of similar assistance in the last **3 years** (1 year for children < 12 years).  
- For children below 12 years, assistance period considered is one year.  
- Income proof may be an income certificate, BPL card, MGNREGA card, disability pension card, or notarized affidavit where certificates are unavailable.

## Required documents
1. UDID card (or enrollment number) and Disability Certificate (≥ 40 %).  
2. Aadhaar number / Aadhaar enrollment ID.  
3. Income certificate (Revenue Agency, BPL card, MGNREGA card, Disability Pension Card, or notarized affidavit).  
4. For orphanage or half‑way home residents – certification from District Collector or head of the organization.  
5. Joint disability certificate (if required) signed by headmaster/principal, government doctor (PHC/CHC), local SSA authority, or ALIMCO representative.  
6. For cochlear implant – referral from nodal agency (AYJNISHD Mumbai) and empanelled hospital details.  

## How to apply
1. **Verify eligibility** – ensure UDID, disability certificate (≥ 40 %), Aadhaar, and income limits are met.  
2. **Collect documents** – gather all required certificates and affidavits.  
3. **Approach an implementing agency** – select an agency with qualified staff and ISO‑certified fabrication facilities (as per clause 5.02).  
4. **Submit application** – provide documents to the agency; the agency will verify and forward the request to the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
5. **Assessment & recommendation** – expert committee evaluates the required aid/appliance and determines the subsidy amount.  
6. **Approval & procurement** – once approved, the agency procures the device (ALIMCO/NIs/CRCs for most items; AYJNISHD for cochlear implants).  
7. **Fitment & post‑fitment care** – agency ensures proper fitting, training, and follow‑up care.  
8. **Travel & lodging reimbursement** – submit receipts for travel (≤ Rs. 250) and boarding/lodging (≤ Rs. 100/day, max 15 days) to receive reimbursement.
