## Overview
The ADIP Scheme, operational since 1981, assists needy persons with disabilities in procuring durable, scientifically manufactured aids and appliances to promote physical, social, and psychological rehabilitation. Grants‑in‑aid are released to implementing agencies such as ALIMCO, National Institutes, Regional Centres, District Disability Rehabilitation Centres, State Corporations, and NGOs for purchase and distribution of these devices.

## Benefits
- Provision of certified assistive devices and appliances to improve independent functioning and reduce secondary disabilities.  
- Conduct of corrective surgeries, where required, before the provision of an assistive device.  
- Grants‑in‑aid to implementing agencies for procurement and distribution of aids.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be a person with disability (PwD) in need of an assistive device.  
- Must meet the scheme’s certification requirements for the specific aid/appliance.  
- Must comply with any additional eligibility stipulated in the scheme’s revised guidelines (last revised w.e.f. 26‑09‑2024).  

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability and certification of the required aid/appliance.  
2. Aadhaar Card (required for both adults and children as per Gazette notifications dated 29‑02‑2024).  
3. Any additional documents specified in the scheme’s guidelines or by the implementing agency.  

## How to apply
1. Visit the **Arjun Portal** and register as an individual applicant.  
2. Fill in the application form for the desired assistive device, attaching required documents (disability certificate, Aadhaar, etc.).  
3. Submit the application; the portal will generate a reference number.  
4. The implementing agency reviews the application, conducts any required corrective surgery, and approves the grant‑in‑aid.  
5. The approved aid/appliance is procured and delivered to the beneficiary.
