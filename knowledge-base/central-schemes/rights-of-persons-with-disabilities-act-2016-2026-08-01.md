## Overview
The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 is a comprehensive legislation that aims to protect the rights of persons with disabilities in India. The Act provides for the empowerment of persons with disabilities and ensures their equal participation in society. The Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) is responsible for implementing the provisions of the Act.

## Benefits
- Protection of rights of persons with disabilities
- Empowerment of persons with disabilities
- Equal participation in society
- Access to education, employment, and healthcare
- Provision of assistive devices and accessibility features

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities as defined under the Act
- Indian citizens
- Persons with disabilities who require assistance or support

## Required documents
1. Disability certificate
2. Identity proof
3. Address proof

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the official website of the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)
Step 2: Fill out the online application form
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Submit the application
