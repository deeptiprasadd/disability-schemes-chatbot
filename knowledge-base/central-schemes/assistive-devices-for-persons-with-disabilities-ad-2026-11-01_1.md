## Overview
The ADIP Scheme provides assistive devices, aids, and kits to persons with disabilities, including multiple disabilities, leprosy‑cured persons, and newly recognized disabilities under the RPWD Act, 2016. The scheme is administered by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities and is funded through grant‑in‑aid to implementing agencies.

## Benefits
- Provision of suitable assistive devices/kits/learning material as recommended by the Expert Committee.
- Assistive Daily Living (ADL) kits for leprosy‑cured persons.
- Periodic revision of the list of eligible devices within the financial ceiling prescribed by the Expert Committee.
- 1 % of the scheme budget allocated for information, education, and communication activities, including a mobile app, MIS portal, and third‑party evaluation.

## Eligibility criteria
- Beneficiaries must be persons with disabilities as defined under the RPWD Act, 2016 (including multiple disabilities and leprosy‑cured persons).
- Implementation agencies must obtain a certificate of the beneficiary’s monthly income from the competent authority.
- Beneficiaries must not have received similar aid from any other source in the last three years (one year for children below 12 years).

## Required documents
1. Certificate of monthly income issued by the concerned competent authority.  
2. Undertaking from the beneficiary confirming non‑receipt of similar aid in the stipulated period.  
3. For implementing agencies: registration certificates, PAN/Aadhaar details, audited accounts, utilization certificate, and other proforma/annexures as prescribed.

## How to apply
1. **Through Camp Activity** – Implementing agencies assess beneficiaries in district‑level camps and distribute devices.  
2. **Through Headquarter Activity** – Beneficiaries approach National Institutes/CRCs/ALIMCO/DDRCs or approved NGOs at their headquarters.  
3. **Through Mobile App** – Beneficiaries register or request repair via the department’s mobile app; requests are forwarded to the nearest implementing agency.  
4. **Through MIS/ADIP Web Portal** – Online requests are submitted on the portal; devices are supplied within six months of registration.  
5. Implementing agencies verify documents, assess eligibility, and distribute the approved assistive devices.
