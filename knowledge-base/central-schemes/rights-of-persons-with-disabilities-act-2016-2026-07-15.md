## Overview
The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 is a comprehensive legislation that aims to protect the rights of persons with disabilities in India. The Act provides for the empowerment of persons with disabilities and ensures their full participation in society. It also establishes the National Commission for Persons with Disabilities and the State Commissions for Persons with Disabilities to oversee the implementation of the Act.

## Benefits
- Protection of rights of persons with disabilities
- Empowerment of persons with disabilities
- Full participation in society
- Establishment of National and State Commissions for Persons with Disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities as defined under the Act
- Indian citizens

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability
2. Proof of age
3. Proof of income
4. Proof of residence

## How to apply
Step by step instructions are not provided in the given text. However, persons with disabilities can contact the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) or the National Commission for Persons with Disabilities for more information on how to apply for benefits under the Act.
