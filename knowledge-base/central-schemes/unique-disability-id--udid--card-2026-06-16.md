## Overview
The Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card is a card issued by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) to persons with disabilities.

## Benefits
- Unique ID for persons with disabilities
- Access to various schemes and services

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities are eligible for the UDID card
- Specific eligibility criteria may vary depending on the type of disability

## Required documents
1. Disability certificate
2. ID proof
3. Address proof

## How to apply
Step by step instructions to apply for the UDID card:
1. Visit the official website of the DEPwD
2. Fill the online application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit the application
5. Track the application status
