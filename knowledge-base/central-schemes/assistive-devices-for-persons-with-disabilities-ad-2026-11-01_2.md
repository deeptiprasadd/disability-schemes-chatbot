## Overview
The Assistive Devices for Persons with Disabilities (ADIP) Scheme is a centrally‑funded programme implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India. It aims to provide approved aids and devices to persons with disabilities through implementing agencies that organise camps across the country.
