## Overview
The ADIP Scheme is a Central Sector Scheme administered by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan) under the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment. It provides financial assistance for the purchase and fitting of durable, modern, scientifically‑manufactured aids and assistive devices to Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) to promote their physical, social and psychological rehabilitation.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for purchase of approved aids/appliances (quantum of assistance enhanced in the revised scheme).  
- Cost ceiling for essential surgical correction/intervention prior to fitment:  
  - Rs. 1,500 for speech & hearing impaired  
  - Rs. 3,000 for visually impaired  
  - Rs. 15,000 for orthopedically impaired  

## Eligibility criteria
- Beneficiaries must be Persons with Disabilities as defined in the “Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016” and the “National Trust Act, 1999”.  
- Scheme is applicable for the period of the 15th Finance Commission: 01‑04‑2022 to 31‑03‑2026 (revised effective 01‑04‑2024).  
- Income ceiling and other quantitative limits are revised in the latest scheme (specific figures not stated in the source).  

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability (certificate as per RPwD Act, 2016).  
2. Income certificate (to verify family income ceiling – exact limit not stated).  
3. Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).  
4. Any additional documents prescribed by the implementing agency (not detailed in the source).

## How to apply
1. Approach the designated Implementing Agency in your State/UT (e.g., ALIMCO, CRCs, DDRCs, NGOs, etc.).  
2. Submit the required documents listed above.  
3. The agency will verify eligibility, conduct a needs assessment, and determine the quantum of assistance.  
4. Upon approval, the agency will procure and arrange fitting of the approved aid/appliance.  
5. Post‑fitting care and follow‑up will be provided by the same agency.  
6. Beneficiary details are uploaded on the scheme’s Management Information System (MIS) and displayed on the agency’s website.
