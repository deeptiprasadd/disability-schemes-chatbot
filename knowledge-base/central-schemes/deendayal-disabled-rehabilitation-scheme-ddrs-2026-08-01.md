## Overview
The Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS) is a scheme that provides financial assistance to non-governmental organizations (NGOs) for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities. The scheme is implemented by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD).

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for NGOs for rehabilitation of persons with disabilities
- Support for education, employment, and healthcare of persons with disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- NGOs registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 or the Indian Trusts Act, 1882
- NGOs working for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities

## Required documents
1. Registration certificate of the NGO
2. Audited accounts of the NGO
3. Proposal for the rehabilitation project

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the official website of the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)
Step 2: Fill out the online application form
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Submit the application
