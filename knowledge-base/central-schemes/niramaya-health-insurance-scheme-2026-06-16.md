## Overview
The NIRAMAYA Health Insurance Scheme is a healthcare safety net for people with disabilities, providing coverage for a wide variety of medical needs. It is a necessity for individuals with disabilities, ensuring that medical emergencies do not become financial disasters. The scheme covers various expenses, including OPD and hospitalization expenses, medicines, check-ups, dental care, surgeries, therapies, and transportation costs associated with care or treatment.

## Benefits
- OPD and hospitalization expenses
- Medicines, check-ups, dental care, surgeries
- Therapies (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, etc.)
- Transportation costs associated with care or treatment

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with disabilities (autism, cerebral palsy, intellectual disability, multiple disabilities)
- Valid disability certificate
- UDID/enrollment number

## Required documents
1. Valid disability certificate
2. UDID/enrollment number

Step-by-step instructions are not provided in the given text. However, it is mentioned that individuals with disabilities can apply for the scheme with a valid disability certificate and UDID/enrollment number.
