## Overview
The Deendayal Disabled Rehabilitation Scheme (DDRS) is a scheme established by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) to provide financial assistance to voluntary organizations for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities. The scheme aims to promote the empowerment of persons with disabilities and ensure their full participation in society.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities
- Promotion of empowerment of persons with disabilities
- Full participation in society

## Eligibility criteria
- Voluntary organizations working for the rehabilitation of persons with disabilities
- Indian citizens

## Required documents
1. Proof of registration of the voluntary organization
2. Proof of experience in the field of disability rehabilitation
3. Proof of financial stability
4. Proof of infrastructure and facilities

## How to apply
Step by step instructions are not provided in the given text. However, voluntary organizations can contact the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD) for more information on how to apply for benefits under the scheme.
