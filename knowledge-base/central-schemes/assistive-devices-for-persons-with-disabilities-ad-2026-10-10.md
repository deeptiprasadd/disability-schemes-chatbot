## Overview
The ADIP Scheme provides financial assistance for the procurement of assistive devices and appliances to persons with disabilities (PwDs) in India. It targets Indian citizens with at least 40 % disability, low income, and who have not received similar assistance in the preceding years.

## Benefits
- Full cost assistance for aids/appliances costing up to **Rs. 15,000**.  
- Up to **Rs. 15,000** assistance for items costing between **Rs. 15,001 – Rs. 30,000**.  
- 50 % cost sharing for expensive items above Rs. 30,001 (excluding cochlear implants and motorized tricycles), subject to income ceiling and a 20 % budget limit.  
- **Cochlear implant** support up to **Rs. 7 lakh** (pre‑lingual, 1‑5 yr) or **Rs. 6 lakh** (acquired, 5‑18 yr), covering implant, surgery, therapy, mapping, travel and pre‑implant assessment.  
- Travel allowance up to **Rs. 250** per person (beneficiary + one escort).  
- Boarding & lodging allowance up to **Rs. 100 per day** for a maximum of 15 days for eligible beneficiaries (income ≤ Rs. 22,500 / month).  

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian citizen of any age.  
- Possession of a UDID card (or enrollment number) and a Disability Certificate showing **≥ 40 % disability**.  
- Aadhaar number or enrollment ID as required by the Aadhaar Act, 2016.  
- Monthly household income **≤ Rs. 30,000** (or ≤ Rs. 30,000 for parents/guardians of dependents).  
- No receipt of similar assistance in the last **3 years** (1 year for children < 12 yr).  
- Income proof may be an income certificate, BPL card, MGNREGA card, disability pension card, or notarized affidavit where certificates are unavailable.  

## Required documents
1. UDID card (or enrollment number).  
2. Disability Certificate (issued by prescribed Medical Authority).  
3. Aadhaar number / Aadhaar enrollment ID.  
4. Income certificate / BPL card / MGNREGA card / Disability Pension Card / notarized affidavit.  
5. Joint disability certificate (if required) signed by headmaster/principal, government doctor, local SSA authority, or ALIMCO representative.  
6. For children ≤ 6 yr, temporary disability or developmental delay certificate (if applicable).  

## How to apply
1. **Identify a implementing agency** (preferably one with qualified staff and ISO‑certified fabrication facilities).  
2. Collect all required documents listed above.  
3. Submit the application to the selected implementing agency along with the documents.  
4. The agency will verify eligibility, income ceiling, and prior assistance status.  
5. Upon approval, the agency will procure the required aid/appliance (or coordinate with ALIMCO/NIs/CRCs).  
6. Beneficiary attends the nearest Rehabilitation Centre (or designated centre in NE region) for fitment and post‑fitment care.  
7. If travel or boarding‑lodging assistance is needed, submit a claim for the stipulated amounts.
