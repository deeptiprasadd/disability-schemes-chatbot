## Overview
The National Institute for Empowerment of Persons with Intellectual Disabilities (NIEPID) provides services for persons with intellectual disabilities. NIEPID is dedicated to providing quality services to persons with intellectual disabilities. The institute has three regional centers located in Noida/New Delhi, Kolkata, and Mumbai.

## Benefits
- Model Special Education Center
- Family-based services
- Relief care services
- Indian Test of Intelligence Kit
- Toll-free helpline number

## Eligibility criteria
- Persons with intellectual disabilities
- Families of persons with intellectual disabilities

## Required documents
1. Proof of intellectual disability
2. Proof of residence
3. Proof of income (if applicable)

## How to apply
Step by step instructions:
1. Contact NIEPID through their toll-free helpline number or email.
2. Visit the NIEPID website for more information on services and eligibility criteria.
3. Submit required documents to the nearest NIEPID regional center.
