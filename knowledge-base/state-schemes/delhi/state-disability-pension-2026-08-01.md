## Overview
The State Disability Pension scheme is implemented by the Delhi Social Welfare Department, which is a fusion of state and central government contributions. The scheme provides a subsistence allowance/financial allowance to persons with special needs. The grant is Rs 2500/- per month.

## Benefits
- Rs 2500/- per month as subsistence allowance/financial allowance

## Eligibility criteria
- Age of disabled persons should not be more than 60 years (0 to 60 years)
- Family income should not be more than Rs. 75,000/- p.a. from all sources
- Resident of the National Capital Territory of Delhi for at least 5 years preceding the date of submission of application
- Disability of the applicant should not be less than 40%
- Should have a 'single-operated' account in any Bank or Post Office for receiving the payment through electronic clearing system

## Required documents
1. Proof of residence (any one of the documents listed: copy of Ration Card / Voter Card/ Passport/ Driving License / Birth certificate issued by MCD/Registrar-Births and Deaths/ Insurance policy document/ Immunization card of any family member / Medical records of treatment in Delhi / Electricity Bill/ Water Bill/ Telephone bill/ Gas connection receipt/ Bank passbook/ Caste certificate/ Student I-card/ Service identity card of public/private sector company/established concern/ Freedom fighter identity card / Pension document/ Ex-serviceman dependent certificate/ Property document/ Any other document which clearly shows at least 5 years of residence in Delhi)
2. Copy of disability certificate issued by the Medical Board of the notified Hospitals of Govt. of NCT of Delhi or Govt. of India
3. Self-declaration by the applicant regarding his/ her family income
4. Form recommended by a Gazetted Officer of Central /State Govt. or MP/MLA and accompanying documents attested
5. Witness statements and documents (if no documentary evidence is available for proof of residence)

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the District Social Welfare Office to get the application form
Step 2: Fill the form and attach the required documents
Step 3: Get the form recommended by a Gazetted Officer of Central /State Govt. or MP/MLA
Step 4: Submit the application form to the District Social Welfare Office
