## Overview  
The Disability Pension Scheme offers basic financial assistance to persons with disabilities residing in Uttar Pradesh. It targets individuals with at least 40 % direct disability, mental retardation, or hearing impairment, irrespective of gender.  

## Benefits  
- Monthly pension amount (exact amount not specified in the source).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Direct disability of ≥ 40 % **or** mental retardation **or** hearing impairment.  
- Resident of Uttar Pradesh.

## How to apply  
1. Register your mobile number on the “पुराने आवेदक पोर्टल”.  
2. Verify your Aadhaar online through the portal.  
3. Submit the disability pension application as per the portal’s guidelines.
