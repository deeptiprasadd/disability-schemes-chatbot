## Overview  
The Widow Pension Scheme provides financial assistance to women who have become widows after the death of their husband. The scheme is managed by the Women’s Welfare Department of Uttar Pradesh.  

## Benefits  
- Monthly pension amount (exact amount not specified in the source).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Widow of a resident husband of Uttar Pradesh.  
- Age ≥ 18 years.

## How to apply  
1. Register your mobile number on the “पुराने आवेदक पोर्टल”.  
2. Verify your Aadhaar online through the portal.  
3. Complete the application form for Widow Pension as directed on the portal.
