## Overview  
The National Old Age Pension Scheme, administered by the Uttar Pradesh Social Welfare Department, provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens aged 60 years or above who live below the poverty line. It has been operational since 1994 to enable dignified living for the elderly.  

## Benefits  
- Monthly pension amount (exact amount not specified in the source).  
- Financial support aimed at covering basic living expenses.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Age ≥ 60 years.  
- Income below the poverty line (as stated: “गरीबी रेखा के नीचे जीवन यापन करने वाले”).

## How to apply  
1. Register your mobile number on the “पुराने आवेदक पोर्टल”.  
2. Verify your Aadhaar online through the portal.  
3. Submit the application as per portal instructions.
