## Overview  
The **Economic Assistance Scheme for Purchase and Installation of Aids and Devices** provides financial support to persons with disabilities for acquiring assistive devices and having them installed at their place of residence.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance for purchase of assistive devices.  
- Support for on‑site installation of the devices.
