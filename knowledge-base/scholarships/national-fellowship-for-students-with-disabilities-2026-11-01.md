## Overview  
The National Fellowship provides financial support to students with benchmark disabilities pursuing M.Phil or Ph.D. programmes in Indian universities. It forms part of the six‑component “Scholarships for Students with Disabilities” scheme.  

## Benefits  
- Fellowship amount (exact figure not provided in the source) to cover tuition, stipend, and research expenses.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) and a valid disability certificate.  
- Income ceiling as per the component (not explicitly stated; likely aligns with Top Class Education limit of Rs. 8,00,000).  
- Admission to an Indian university for M.Phil or Ph.D. programme.  
- Must meet the general eligibility conditions applicable to all scheme components.  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian (as per income ceiling).  
3. Admission/offer letter for M.Phil or Ph.D. programme.  
4. Academic transcripts and certificates of previous qualifications.  
5. Research proposal (if required).  
6. Any other documents prescribed by the authority.  

## How to apply  
1. Secure disability and income certificates.  
2. Obtain admission to an Indian university for M.Phil/Ph.D.  
3. Fill out the National Fellowship application form (available online/offline).  
4. Attach all required documents, including research proposal if needed.  
5. Submit the application to the designated authority or upload via the official portal.  
6. Upon verification, the fellowship will be disbursed as per the scheme’s schedule.
