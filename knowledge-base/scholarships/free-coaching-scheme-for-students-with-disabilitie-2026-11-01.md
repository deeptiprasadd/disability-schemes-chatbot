## Overview  
The Free Coaching Scheme offers financially‑assisted coaching to students with benchmark disabilities for recruitment examinations (Group A, B, C posts) and entrance exams for technical and professional courses. It is one of the six components under the central “Scholarships for Students with Disabilities” scheme.  

## Benefits  
- Free coaching at pre‑examination training centres funded by the Central/State Government.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) and a valid disability certificate.  
- Must not be receiving any other stipend from the coaching schemes while receiving Central/State financial assistance for coaching.  
- General eligibility conditions for the overall scheme (income ceiling, single‑child rule, etc.) apply.  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian (as per the component’s income ceiling).  
3. Proof of admission/registration for the desired recruitment or entrance examination.  
4. Any additional documents as prescribed by the coaching authority.  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain disability and income certificates.  
2. Identify the relevant pre‑examination training centre offering free coaching.  
3. Complete the Free Coaching Scheme application form (available from the centre or online).  
4. Submit the form along with required documents to the training centre or designated authority.  
5. Await confirmation of enrollment and commencement of coaching.
