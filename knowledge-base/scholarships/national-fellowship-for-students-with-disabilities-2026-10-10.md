## Overview  
The National Fellowship provides financial support to students with benchmark disabilities undertaking M.Phil or Ph.D. programmes in Indian universities.  

## Benefits  
- Fellowship amount (specific quantum not mentioned).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥40 % certified).  
- Admission to an M.Phil or Ph.D. programme in an Indian university.  
- Income and other conditions (not detailed in the source).  

## Required documents  
1. Disability certificate.  
2. Admission/registration proof for M.Phil/Ph.D.  
3. Income certificate (if required).  
4. Completed application form (not detailed).  

## How to apply  
1. Secure disability certification.  
2. Obtain admission to the desired M.Phil/Ph.D. programme.  
3. Fill the prescribed application form (available on DEPwD portal).  
4. Submit the form with required documents to the designated authority.  
5. Await selection and disbursement of the fellowship.
