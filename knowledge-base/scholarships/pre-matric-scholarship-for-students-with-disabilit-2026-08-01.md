## Overview
The Pre-Matric Scholarship for Students with Disabilities is a central scheme available through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) that aims to empower students with disabilities to pursue a quality education. This initiative is supported by government initiatives and provides critical resources to help students overcome unique challenges. The scholarship is open for applications starting June 1, 2026, and the deadline to apply is August 31, 2026.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance to students with disabilities
- Empowerment to pursue quality education
- Resources to overcome unique challenges

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian nationals with benchmark disability ≥40%
- Valid disability certificate required

## Required documents
1. Valid disability certificate
2. Income certificate

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in
Step 2: Register and create an account
Step 3: Fill out the application form
Step 4: Upload required documents
Step 5: Submit the application

## Overview
The Legrand Empowering Scholarship Program is a private scholarship that aims to empower girl students, including those with disabilities, to pursue higher education. This initiative is supported by Legrand and provides critical resources to help students overcome unique challenges.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance to girl students with disabilities
- Empowerment to pursue higher education
- Resources to overcome unique challenges

## Eligibility criteria
- Open for girl students (including LGBTQ+, differently-abled girls, COVID-affected, single-parent, or orphan students)
- Admission in B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.
- Must have passed Class 12
- Marks requirement: ≥70% in Class 10 & 12 (General)/≥60% (Special categories)
- Family income ≤ ₹5 lakh per annum

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission
2. Mark sheets
3. Income certificate
