## Overview
The Top Class Education Scholarship aims to promote quality higher education among students with disabilities by providing financial support for graduate, post‑graduate or diploma programmes in institutes of excellence notified by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD).

## Benefits
- Reimbursement of tuition fees and non‑refundable charges up to **Rs. 2.00 lakh per annum** (subject to actual amount).  
- Maintenance allowance: **Rs. 3,000 per month** for hostellers, **Rs. 1,500 per month** for day‑scholars.  
- Special allowances (reader, escort, helper, etc.) **Rs. 2,000 per month**.  
- Books & stationery **Rs. 5,000 per annum**.  
- One‑time grant for purchase of a computer/laptop with accessories **Rs. 45,000** (requires bill/receipt).  
- One‑time grant for purchase of aids and assistive devices **Rs. 30,000** (requires bills/vouchers).

## Eligibility criteria
- Student must be a person with disability (any type).  
- Must seek admission to a graduate, post‑graduate degree or diploma in an institute of excellence notified by DEPwD.  
- Must submit proof of age, disability certificate, income certificate of parent, tuition fee receipt, last academic qualification, etc. (required when total scholarship > Rs. 50,000 per annum).  
- 50 % of slots are reserved for girls.  
- Selection based on marks in qualifying examination; ties resolved by higher percentage of disability, then older age.  
- Slots per state allocated proportionally to the state’s PwD population.

## Required documents
1. Recent photograph.  
2. Proof of age.  
3. Disability certificate.  
4. Income certificate of parent/guardian.  
5. Tuition fee receipt.  
6. Last academic qualification certificate.  
7. For computer/laptop purchase: bill/receipt as proof of purchase.  
8. For assistive devices: bills/vouchers as proof of purchase.  

## How to apply
1. Register on the **National Scholarship Portal** (www.scholarships.gov.in).  
2. Fill the online application before the prescribed last date.  
3. Upload all requisite documents in **.pdf** format (if total scholarship > Rs. 50,000 per annum).  
4. If total scholarship < Rs. 50,000 per annum, keep supporting documents with the Head of School/Institution.  
5. The institution must also register on the portal and verify the candidate’s details.  
6. The State nodal officer reviews applications, signs the final list in PFMS, and forwards it for disbursal.  

## Contact
