## Overview  
The Top Class Education Scholarship provides financial assistance to students with benchmark disabilities pursuing graduate, post‑graduate degrees or diplomas in institutes of excellence notified by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  

## Benefits  
- Quantum of financial assistance (exact amount not specified in the source).  
- Covers tuition, maintenance, and other study‑related expenses for the duration of the course.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) and a valid disability certificate.  
- Annual parental/guardian income must not exceed **Rs. 8,00,000**.  
- Admission must be to a institute of excellence notified by DEPwD.  
- General eligibility conditions applicable to all scheme components (single‑child rule, one‑year scholarship per class, no concurrent scholarships) also apply.  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian showing total income ≤ Rs. 8,00,000.  
3. Admission letter / proof of enrollment from a notified institute of excellence.  
4. Academic certificates of previous qualifications.  
5. Any other documents as prescribed.  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain disability and income certificates.  
2. Secure admission to a DEPwD‑notified institute of excellence.  
3. Download the Top Class Education Scholarship application form.  
4. Fill in the form with all required details and attach the documents listed above.  
5. Submit the application to the designated authority or upload it on the official portal.  
6. Upon verification, the scholarship amount will be disbursed as per the scheme’s mode of disbursal.
