## Overview
The PM YASASVI Central Sector Scheme provides top‑class education scholarships to school‑going students belonging to OBC, EBC and DNT categories. The scheme is currently open for fresh applications for the academic year 2026‑27.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for school education to eligible OBC, EBC and DNT students.  
- One‑time registration (OTR) number linked to Aadhaar simplifies future applications.

## Eligibility criteria
- Student must belong to OBC, EBC or DNT category.  
- Student must **not** be a resident of Rajasthan, Kerala or Karnataka (students from these states must wait).  
- All disabled students must first log in to the UDID portal, give consent to share UDID details, and then submit the application on NSP.

## Required documents
Not stated in the source.

## How to apply
1. Log in to the **UDID portal** and provide consent to share UDID details (mandatory for disabled students).  
2. Generate or obtain your **One Time Registration (OTR)** number (14‑digit) using Aadhaar/EID.  
3. Log in to the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** and select the PM YASASVI scheme.  
4. Fill in the application form with required personal and academic details.  
5. Submit the application before the closing date (not specified in the excerpt).
