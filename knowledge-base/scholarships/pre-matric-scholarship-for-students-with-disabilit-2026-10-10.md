## Overview  
The Pre‑matric Scholarship is a central‑sector umbrella scheme that provides financial assistance to students with benchmark disabilities studying in Classes IX and X. It aims to promote inclusive education and reduce the economic burden on families of disabled children.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance (amount not specified in the source) for the academic year.  
- One‑year scholarship per class; repeaters are not eligible for a second year.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national.  
- Student must have a benchmark disability (≥40 % disability) certified by a competent authority.  
- Parents/guardian’s total annual income must not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000**.  
- Not more than two children with disabilities from the same parents can receive the benefit (twins are an exception).  
- The student must not be receiving any other scholarship/stipend from the central scheme (can accept free lodging or ad‑hoc help).  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate issued by the certifying authority.  
2. Income certificate of parents/guardian showing total annual income ≤ Rs. 2,50,000.  
3. Academic records for Class VIII/IX as applicable.  
4. Application form (not detailed in the source).  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain a valid disability certificate.  
2. Collect the required income and academic documents.  
3. Fill the prescribed application form (available on the DEPwD portal).  
4. Submit the application to the designated authority/college as instructed in the scheme notice.  
5. Await selection and disbursal of the scholarship for the academic year.
