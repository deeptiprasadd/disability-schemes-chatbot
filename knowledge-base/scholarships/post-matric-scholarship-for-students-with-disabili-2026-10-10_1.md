## Overview
The Post‑Matric Scholarship for Students with Disabilities provides financial assistance to eligible disabled students pursuing recognized post‑matriculation or post‑secondary courses in India. The scheme covers maintenance, disability allowance, reimbursement of compulsory non‑refundable fees, and a book allowance.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (rate varies by course group and hosteller/day scholar status)  
- Disability allowance (₹4,000 for visually impaired and intellectual disabilities; ₹2,000 for hearing impaired, physically disabled, and other disabilities)  
- Reimbursement of compulsory non‑refundable fees up to ₹1.50 lakh per annum  
- Book allowance of ₹1,500 per annum  

## Eligibility criteria
- Must have passed Matriculation, Higher Secondary, or any higher examination of a recognized university/board.  
- Must be a student of a recognized post‑matriculation or post‑secondary course (including correspondence/distant education).  
- Income of the student combined with parents/guardian must not exceed the prescribed ceiling.  
- Must possess a valid disability certificate for one of the listed disability types.  
- Must not be simultaneously enrolled in two different courses (except where permitted by the educational authority).  

## Required documents
1. Recent passport‑size photograph  
2. Proof of age (birth certificate or school records)  
3. Disability certificate specifying type of disability  
4. Income certificate of parent/guardian  
5. Tuition/fees receipt (if scholarship amount > ₹50,000 per annum)  
6. Last academic qualification certificate  
7. Any other documents prescribed in the online application form  

## How to apply
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal: **www.scholarships.gov.in**.  
2. Register/login and select the Post‑Matric Scholarship for Disabled Students.  
3. Fill in the online application form with personal, academic, and financial details.  
4. Upload all required documents in **.pdf** format (if total scholarship amount > ₹50,000 per annum).  
5. Submit the application before the last date announced for your state.  
6. The institution where you are studying must also register on the portal and verify your details.  
7. The State nodal officer will review applications; final selection is made on the portal based on merit, disability percentage, and age.  
8. Upon approval, the scholarship amount is disbursed through the Public Finance Management System (PFMS).
