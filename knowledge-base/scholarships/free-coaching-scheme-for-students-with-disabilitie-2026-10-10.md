## Overview  
The Free Coaching Scheme offers tuition‑free coaching to students with benchmark disabilities for recruitment examinations (Group A, B, C) and entrance exams for technical and professional courses.  

## Benefits  
- Free coaching for specified competitive examinations (exact number of sessions or duration not specified).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥40 % certified).  
- Enrolment in a recognized pre‑examination training centre funded by the Central or State Government.  
- Not receiving a stipend from the coaching scheme while already receiving financial assistance from other coaching programmes.  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Proof of enrollment in a government‑funded coaching centre.  
3. Income certificate (if applicable).  
4. Completed application form (not detailed).  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain a certified disability certificate.  
2. Register with a government‑approved coaching centre.  
3. Submit the application form along with required documents to the coaching centre or designated authority.  
4. Attend the coaching sessions as per the schedule.
