## Overview
The PM‑USP Special Scholarship Scheme provides merit‑based financial aid to students from Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. Renewal applications for the academic year 2026‑27 are open on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## Benefits
- Merit‑based scholarship support specifically for students of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be a student from Jammu & Kashmir or Ladakh who previously received the PM‑USP Special Scholarship.  
- Renewal applications must be submitted by **31‑10‑2026**.  
- Institute verification deadline: **15‑11‑2026**; L2 verification deadline: **30‑11‑2026**.  

## Required documents
Not stated in the source.

## How to apply
1. Access the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**.  
2. Choose the “PM USP Special Scholarship Scheme For Jammu Kashmir And Ladakh” renewal option.  
3. Fill in the required details and update any academic information.  
4. Submit the renewal application before 31‑10‑2026.  
5. Ensure institute verification is completed by 15‑11‑2026 and L2 verification by 30‑11‑2026.
