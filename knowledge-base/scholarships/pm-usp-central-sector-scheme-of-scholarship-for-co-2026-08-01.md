## Overview
The PM-USP – Central Sector Scheme Of Scholarship For College And University Students (CSSS) is a merit-based scholarship scheme for college and university students. The scheme is currently open for renewal applications on the National Scholarship Portal. The portal is open for the academic year 2026-27.

## Benefits
- Merit-based scholarship for college and university students
- Opportunity to apply for one merit-based scholarship scheme and one or more welfare-based scholarship schemes

## Eligibility criteria
- Students must meet the scheme's eligibility criteria
- Students can apply for one merit-based scholarship scheme and one or more welfare-based scholarship schemes

## Required documents
1. Aadhaar card
2. Aadhaar enrollment number (EID)

## How to apply
Step 1: Register on the National Scholarship Portal
Step 2: Generate a One Time Registration (OTR) number using Aadhaar/Aadhaar enrollment number (EID)
Step 3: Apply for the scholarship scheme
Step 4: Verify the application through the institute and L2 verification process
