## Overview
The Post‑Matric Scholarship for Persons with Disabilities provides financial assistance to eligible students pursuing recognized post‑matriculation or post‑secondary courses in India. The scheme covers maintenance, disability, fee reimbursement, and book allowances, and is administered through the National Scholarship Portal.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (rate varies by course group and hosteller/day scholar status)  
- Disability allowance (amount varies by type of disability)  
- Reimbursement of compulsory non‑refundable fees up to **Rs. 1.50 lakh per annum**  
- Book allowance of **Rs. 1,500 per annum**

## Eligibility criteria
- Must have passed Matriculation, Higher Secondary, or any higher examination of a recognized university/board.  
- Must be enrolled in a recognized post‑matriculation or post‑secondary course (including professional, technical, diploma, degree, ITI, etc.).  
- Income of the student combined with parents/guardian must not exceed the maximum prescribed income ceiling.  
- Must possess a valid disability certificate for one of the listed disability types.  
- For courses in Group I, a first‑time failure does not disqualify renewal; subsequent failures require the student to bear expenses.  
- 50 % of slots are reserved for girls; selection also considers marks, disability percentage, and age in case of ties.

## Required documents
1. Photograph (PDF)  
2. Proof of age (PDF)  
3. Disability certificate (PDF)  
4. Income certificate of parent/guardian (PDF)  
5. Tuition fees receipt (PDF) – required if total scholarship > Rs. 50,000 per annum  
6. Last academic qualification certificate (PDF)  
7. Any other documents prescribed in the application format

## How to apply
1. Visit the **National Scholarship Portal**: [www.scholarships.gov.in](http://www.scholarships.gov.in).  
2. Register/login and fill in the online application form before the last date.  
3. Upload all required documents in **.pdf** format.  
4. If the scholarship amount is **≤ Rs. 50,000 per annum**, keep supporting documents with the Head of School/Institution.  
5. The institution registers on the portal and verifies the candidate’s details.  
6. The State nodal officer reviews applications; final selection is made on the portal based on merit and slot availability.  
7. The selected list is digitally signed in the Public Finance Management System (PFMS) for disbursement.
