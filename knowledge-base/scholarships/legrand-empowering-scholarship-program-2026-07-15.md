## Overview
The Legrand Empowering Scholarship Program is a scholarship scheme for girl students, including those with disabilities, to pursue higher education. The scheme is open for students who have passed Class 12 and have secured admission in B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for higher education
- Empowerment of girl students with disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Open for girl students (including LGBTQ+, differently-abled girls, COVID-affected, single-parent, or orphan students)
- Admission in B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.
- Must have passed Class 12
- Marks requirement: ≥70% in Class 10 & 12 (General)/≥60% (Special categories)
- Family income ≤ ₹5 lakh per annum

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission
2. Proof of passing Class 12
3. Income certificate
4. Disability certificate (if applicable)

Step 2: Fill out the online application form
Step 3: Upload required documents

## Overview
HDFC Bank Parivartan’s ECSS Programme is a scholarship scheme for Indian nationals studying in Class 1–12, diploma, ITI, polytechnic, UG, PG. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from low-income families.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for education
- Empowerment of students from low-income families

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian nationals studying in Class 1–12, diploma, ITI, polytechnic, UG, PG
- Minimum 55% in the last qualifying exam
- Family income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh per annum

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission
2. Proof of passing the last qualifying exam
3. Income certificate

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the official website of HDFC Bank ECSS at hdfcbankecss.com or the Buddy4Study portal at buddy4study.com
Step 2: Fill out the online application form

## Overview
The Indus Towers Scholarship Program is a scholarship scheme for Indian nationals with benchmark disability ≥40%. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students with disabilities pursuing higher education.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for higher education
- Empowerment of students with disabilities

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian nationals with benchmark disability ≥40%
- Category I: Domicile of Northeast states (Nagaland, Assam, etc.)
- Category II: Students from the rest of India studying in Institutes of National Importance (IITs, NITs, AIIMS, etc.)
- Valid disability certificate required

## Required documents

2. Proof of disability
3. Income certificate
