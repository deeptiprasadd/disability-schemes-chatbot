## Overview
The Top Class Education scheme provides financial support to students with disabilities pursuing graduate, post‑graduate or diploma programmes in institutes of excellence notified by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD). The aim is to promote quality higher education for persons with disabilities.

## Benefits
- Reimbursement of tuition fees and non‑refundable charges up to **Rs. 2.00 lakh per annum** (subject to actual amount).  
- Maintenance allowance: **Rs. 3,000 per month** for hostellers, **Rs. 1,500 per month** for day‑scholars.  
- Special allowances (reader, escort, helper, etc.) **Rs. 2,000 per month**.  
- Books & stationery **Rs. 5,000 per annum**.  
- One‑time grant for computer/laptop with accessories **Rs. 45,000** (requires purchase proof).  
- One‑time grant for aids and assistive devices **Rs. 30,000** (requires bills/vouchers).

## Eligibility criteria
- Student must have a disability certificate (any type).  
- Must be admitted to a graduate, post‑graduate or diploma programme in a **DEPwD‑notified institute of excellence**.  
- Income certificate of the parent required if total scholarship amount exceeds **Rs. 50,000 per annum**.  
- 50 % of the slots are reserved for female candidates.  
- Selection based on (i) state‑wise slot allocation, (ii) percentage of marks, (iii) percentage of disability (as tie‑breaker), and (iv) age (older candidate preferred if still tied).  
- Candidates must maintain satisfactory academic progress and conduct; false statements or change of course subject may lead to cancellation and blacklisting.

## Required documents
1. Recent passport‑size photograph.  
2. Proof of age (birth certificate / school certificate).  
3. Disability certificate.  
4. Income certificate of the parent/guardian (if scholarship > Rs. 50,000/‑annum).  
5. Tuition fee receipt.  
6. Last academic qualification certificate.  
7. Bills/receipts for laptop/computer purchase (if claiming Rs. 45,000 grant).  
8. Bills/vouchers for aids and assistive devices (if claiming Rs. 30,000 grant).  

*(All documents must be uploaded in .pdf format; otherwise the application may be rejected.)*

## How to apply
1. Visit the **National Scholarship Portal** – www.scholarships.gov.in.  
2. Register (or log in) and fill the online application before the prescribed last date.  
3. Upload all required documents in .pdf format.  
4. The institution where the candidate is studying must also register on the portal and verify the applicant’s details.  
5. The State nodal officer reviews applications, signs the final list in the Public Finance Management System (PFMS), and forwards it for disbursal.  
6. If the total scholarship amount is **≤ Rs. 50,000/‑annum**, supporting documents may be kept with the Head of School/Institution; otherwise they must be uploaded.
