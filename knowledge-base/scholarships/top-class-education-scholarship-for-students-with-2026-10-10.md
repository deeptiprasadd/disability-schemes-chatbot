## Overview  
The Top Class Education Scholarship provides financial assistance to students with benchmark disabilities pursuing graduate, post‑graduate degrees or diplomas in institutes of excellence notified by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  

## Benefits  
- Quantum of financial assistance (exact amount not specified).  
- Covers tuition, maintenance, and other study‑related expenses for the duration of the course.  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥40 % certified).  
- Parents/guardian’s total annual income must not exceed **Rs. 8,00,000**.  
- Admission to a notified institute of excellence.  
- Same‑parent limit of two eligible children (twins exempt).  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate showing annual income ≤ Rs. 8,00,000.  
3. Admission letter from a notified institute of excellence.  
4. Completed application form (not detailed).  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain disability and income certificates.  
2. Secure admission to a notified institute.  
3. Fill the prescribed application form (available on DEPwD portal).  
4. Submit the form and supporting documents to the designated authority.  
5. Await award and subsequent disbursement.
