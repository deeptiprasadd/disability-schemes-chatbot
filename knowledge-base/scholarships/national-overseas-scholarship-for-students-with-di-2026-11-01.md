## Overview  
The National Overseas Scholarship supports students with benchmark disabilities who wish to pursue Master’s or Ph.D. programmes abroad at recognized institutions. It is one of the six components of the central “Scholarships for Students with Disabilities” scheme.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance covering tuition, living expenses, and other study‑related costs for overseas education (exact quantum not specified).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) and a valid disability certificate.  
- Income ceiling as per the component (not explicitly stated; assume same as Top Class Education i.e., Rs. 8,00,000 unless otherwise specified).  
- Admission to a recognized foreign university for a Master’s or Ph.D. programme.  
- Must satisfy the general eligibility conditions applicable to all scheme components.  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian (as per income ceiling).  
3. Admission/offer letter from the foreign university.  
4. Academic transcripts and certificates of previous qualifications.  
5. Passport copy and visa details (if applicable).  
6. Any additional documents prescribed by the authority.  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain disability and income certificates.  
2. Secure admission to a recognized overseas institution.  
3. Complete the National Overseas Scholarship application form (available on the official portal).  
4. Attach all required documents, including passport and visa details.  
5. Submit the application to the designated authority or upload it online.  
6. After verification, the scholarship will be disbursed as per the scheme’s guidelines.
