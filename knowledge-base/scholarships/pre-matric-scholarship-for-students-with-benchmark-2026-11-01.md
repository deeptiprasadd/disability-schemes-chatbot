## Overview
The Pre‑Matric Scholarship aims to support regular, full‑time students with benchmark disabilities studying in classes IX and X in government‑recognized schools. It seeks to reduce drop‑out rates and promote inclusive education at the elementary level.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (Rs 500 per month for day scholars, Rs 800 per month for hostellers)  
- Book grant (Rs 1,000 per annum)  
- Disability allowance (varying by disability type, e.g., Rs 4,000 per annum for visually impaired)

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be a regular, full‑time student in class IX or X of a government school or a school recognized by the Government or a Central/State Board.  
- Must belong to a benchmark disability category (visual, hearing, physical, intellectual, or other).  
- Income declaration required as per guidelines (self‑employed parents: certificate from Revenue Officer not below Tehsildar; employed parents: consolidated certificate).  
- 50 % of slots reserved for girl candidates; remaining slots may be filled by male candidates if female slots are unfilled.  

## Required documents
1. Income declaration certificate (as per Note 2).  
2. Aadhaar and Aadhaar EID (mandatory from 14 Mar 2017).  
3. Unique Disability Identity Card (UDID) registration proof.  
4. Certificate from Head of Institution confirming residence if staying in an approved place of residence.  
5. Academic records and disability certificate.  

## How to apply
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal (www.scholarships.gov.in).  
2. Upload required documents in **.pdf** format.  
3. Submit the application before the prescribed last date.  
4. The institution registers on the portal and verifies the candidate’s details.  
5. The State nodal officer reviews and digitally signs the final list on the PFMS portal for DBT disbursal.  
6. Selection is based on merit (marks, disability percentage, age) and reservation criteria.

## Overview
The Post‑Matric Scholarship provides financial assistance to students with benchmark disabilities pursuing education from class XI up to postgraduate levels, including diploma, degree, and master’s programmes, to reduce dropout rates and improve enrolment.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (rates not specified in the provided excerpt)  
- Book grant (rates not specified)  
- Disability allowance (rates not specified)  

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be enrolled in recognized post‑matriculation or post‑secondary courses (classes XI, XII, diploma/certificate, bachelor’s, or master’s) at institutions approved by UGC/AICTE.  
- Excludes training courses such as Aircraft Maintenance Engineer, Private Pilot License, and certain military training programmes.  
- Income declaration and documentation requirements follow the same rules as the Pre‑Matric scheme.  
- 50 % of slots reserved for girl candidates; unfilled female slots may be allocated to male candidates.  

## Required documents
1. Income declaration certificate (as per Note 2).  
2. Aadhaar and Aadhaar EID.  
3. Unique Disability Identity Card (UDID) registration proof.  
4. Academic transcripts and proof of enrollment in a recognized institution.  
5. Disability certificate.  

## How to apply
1. Access the National Scholarship Portal (www.scholarships.gov.in).  
2. Upload all required documents in **.pdf** format.  
3. Submit the application before the deadline announced on the portal.  
4. Institution verifies the applicant’s details on the portal.  
5. State nodal officer processes applications, signs the final list on PFMS portal, and scholarship amounts are transferred via DBT.  
6. Selection follows merit criteria (marks, disability percentage, age) and reservation for girls.
