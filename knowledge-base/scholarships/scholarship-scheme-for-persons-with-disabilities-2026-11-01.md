## Overview
The scheme provides a maintenance allowance scholarship to students with disabilities pursuing higher education. The allowance is payable from the month of admission (or 1 April, whichever is later) until the examinations are completed, subject to renewal conditions.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance payable monthly from admission/1 April until completion of examinations, including holidays.  
- Continuity of allowance on renewal if the course of study remains continuous.

## Eligibility criteria
- The scholar must be a person with disability as defined by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
- The course of study must be approved by the Department; any change in course requires prior approval.  
- Scholarship may be discontinued and refunded if the scholar discontinues studies during the year.  
- Admission after the 20th day of a month results in payment starting from the following month.

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability (certificate issued by a recognized medical authority).  
2. Admission letter from a notified institute of excellence.  
3. Academic records and progress reports as required by the Department.  
4. Any additional documents specified by the Department for scholarship renewal.

## How to apply
1. Obtain a disability certificate and ensure it is registered with the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
2. Apply for admission to a listed Institute of Excellence (see Annexure‑I).  
3. Submit the scholarship application along with required documents to the Department before the start of the academic year.  
4. If changing the course of study, seek prior approval from the Department.  
5. For renewal, submit a renewal application before the end of the current scholarship year, providing proof of continuous study.
