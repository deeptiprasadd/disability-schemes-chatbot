## Overview
The scheme provides a maintenance allowance scholarship to students with disabilities for the duration of their course of study, subject to approval and compliance with the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPD) guidelines.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance payable from 1 April or from the month of admission (whichever is later) until the examinations are completed, including holidays.  
- Continuation of the allowance for renewal scholars from the month following the last paid month of the previous year, provided the course is continuous.

## Eligibility criteria
- The scholar must be a person with disability as defined by the DEPD.  
- The course of study must be approved by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
- If the scholar changes the course of study, prior approval from the DEPD is required.  
- Discontinuation of studies may lead to a refund of the scholarship amount at the discretion of the DEPD.

## Required documents
1. Proof of disability (as per DEPD guidelines).  
2. Admission letter/confirmation from the institute.  
3. Any additional documents required for approval of the course of study by the DEPD.

## How to apply
1. Obtain admission to a recognized institute listed in the Annexure‑I of the scheme.  
2. Submit the disability certificate and admission proof to the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.  
3. Await approval of the course of study from the DEPD.  
4. Upon approval, the maintenance allowance will be credited as per the schedule (from 1 April or month of admission, whichever is later).  
5. For renewal, submit renewal application before the end of the current scholarship year, indicating continuity of the course.
