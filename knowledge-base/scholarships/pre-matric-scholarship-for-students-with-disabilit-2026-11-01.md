## Overview  
The Pre‑matric Scholarship supports students with benchmark disabilities studying in Classes IX and X. It is part of the central‑sector umbrella scheme “Scholarships for Students with Disabilities” and aims to promote inclusive education.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance for the academic year (one‑year only).  
- The amount is specified under “Scholarship Amount” in the scheme (exact figure not stated in the source).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national.  
- Student must have a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) with a valid disability certificate as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.  
- Annual parental/guardian income must not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000**.  
- Not more than two children with disabilities from the same parents may receive the benefit (twins are an exception).  
- The student must not be receiving any other scholarship/stipend (except free lodging or ad‑hoc assistance).  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate issued by a competent authority.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian showing total income ≤ Rs. 2,50,000.  
3. Academic records (Class VIII mark sheet / certificate of admission to Class IX).  
4. Any other document prescribed by the implementing authority.  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain the disability certificate and income certificate.  
2. Download the application form from the official portal or collect it from the designated authority.  
3. Fill in the form with accurate personal, academic, and financial details.  
4. Attach the required documents (listed above).  
5. Submit the completed application to the concerned District/State authority or upload it on the online portal as instructed.  
6. Await verification and selection; the scholarship, if awarded, will be disbursed for one academic year.
