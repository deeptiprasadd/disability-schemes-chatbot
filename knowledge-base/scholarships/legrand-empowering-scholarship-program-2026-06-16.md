## Overview
The Legrand Empowering Scholarship Program is a scholarship scheme for girl students, including those with disabilities, to pursue higher education. The scheme aims to empower girls from underprivileged backgrounds to achieve their academic aspirations. The program is open for students who have passed Class 12 and are admitted to B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for higher education
- Empowerment of girl students from underprivileged backgrounds
- Opportunity to pursue quality education

## Eligibility criteria
- Open for girl students, including LGBTQ+, differently-abled girls, COVID-affected, single-parent, or orphan students
- Admission in B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.
- Must have passed Class 12
- Marks requirement: ≥70% in Class 10 & 12 (General)/≥60% (Special categories)
- Family income ≤ ₹5 lakh per annum

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission to B.Tech, B.E., B.Arch., B.B.A., B.Com., or B.Sc.
2. Class 10 and 12 mark sheets
3. Proof of family income

## How to apply
Step 1: Visit the Buddy4Study portal at buddy4study.com
Step 2: Fill out the online application form for the Legrand Empowering Scholarship Program
Step 3: Upload required documents
Step 4: Submit the application

## Overview
HDFC Bank Parivartan’s ECSS Programme is a scholarship scheme for Indian nationals studying in Class 1–12, diploma, ITI, polytechnic, UG, PG. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance to students from underprivileged backgrounds to achieve their academic aspirations.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for education
- Opportunity to pursue quality education

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian nationals studying in Class 1–12, diploma, ITI, polytechnic, UG, PG
- Minimum 55% in the last qualifying exam
- Family income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh per annum

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission to current course
2. Mark sheet of last qualifying exam
3. Proof of family income

## Benefits
- Financial assistance for education
- Opportunity to pursue quality education

## Eligibility criteria
- Indian nationals with benchmark disability ≥40%
- Category I: Domicile of Northeast states (Nagaland, Assam, etc.)
- Category II: Students from the rest of India studying in Institutes of National Importance (IITs, NITs, AIIMS, etc.)
- Valid disability certificate required

## Required documents
1. Proof of admission to current course
2. Valid disability certificate
3. Proof of domicile (for Category I)
