## Overview  
The Post‑matric Scholarship assists students with benchmark disabilities pursuing education from Class XI up to Post‑Graduate Degree/Diploma. It is one of six components under the central‑sector “Scholarships for Students with Disabilities” scheme.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance covering tuition, maintenance allowance, disability allowance, reimbursement of compulsory non‑refundable fees, and book allowance (exact amounts not specified in the source).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥ 40 % disability) and a valid disability certificate.  
- Annual parental/guardian income must not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000**.  
- No more than two disabled children of the same parents may receive the benefit (twins are an exception).  
- Scholarship is granted for one academic year only; repeaters are not eligible for a second year.  
- The student must not be receiving any other scholarship/stipend (except free lodging or ad‑hoc assistance).  

## Required documents  
1. Valid disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate of parent(s)/guardian showing total income ≤ Rs. 2,50,000.  
3. Academic transcripts / proof of admission for the relevant course (Class XI onward).  
4. Any additional documents prescribed by the authority.  

## How to apply  
1. Secure the disability and income certificates.  
2. Obtain the Post‑matric Scholarship application form (online or offline).  
3. Complete the form with personal, academic, and financial details.  
4. Attach all required documents.  
5. Submit the application to the designated authority (district/state level) or upload via the official portal.  
6. After verification, the scholarship will be disbursed for the academic year.
