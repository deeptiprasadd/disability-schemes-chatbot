## Overview
The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is a platform that offers various scholarship schemes to students. Students can apply for one merit-based scholarship scheme and one or more welfare-based scholarship schemes from the Academic Year 2026-27, as per scheme eligibility criteria. The portal is open for the academic year 2026-27 from 1st June 2026 onwards.

## Benefits
- Merit-based scholarship
- Welfare-based scholarship
- Simplified scholarship application process through One Time Registration (OTR)

## Eligibility criteria
- Students must meet the eligibility criteria for the specific scholarship scheme they are applying for
- Students can apply for one merit-based scholarship scheme and one or more welfare-based scholarship schemes

## Required documents
1. Aadhaar card
2. Aadhaar Enrollment number (EID)

## How to apply
Step 1: Generate a One Time Registration (OTR) number using Aadhaar/Aadhaar Enrollment number (EID)
Step 2: Fill out the scholarship application form on the National Scholarship Portal
Step 3: Submit the application form and required documents

## Contact
For assistance, students can visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or contact the NSP support team through the portal.
