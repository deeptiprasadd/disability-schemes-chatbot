## Overview  
The National Overseas Scholarship enables students with benchmark disabilities to pursue Master’s or Ph.D. programmes abroad at recognized foreign institutions.  

## Benefits  
- Financial assistance for tuition, living expenses, and other study‑related costs (exact amounts not provided).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥40 % certified).  
- Admission to a recognized foreign university for Master’s or Ph.D. programme.  
- Income ceiling and other conditions (not specified in the source).  

## Required documents  
1. Disability certificate.  
2. Admission/offer letter from the foreign institution.  
3. Income certificate (if applicable).  
4. Completed application form (not detailed).  

## How to apply  
1. Obtain disability certification.  
2. Secure admission to an overseas university.  
3. Complete the prescribed application form (available on DEPwD website).  
4. Submit the form with supporting documents to the designated authority.  
5. Follow the selection process and receive the scholarship if awarded.
