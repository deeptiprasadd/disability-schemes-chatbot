## Overview
The PM‑USP Central Sector Scheme offers merit‑based scholarships to college and university students across India. Renewal applications for the academic year 2026‑27 are currently open on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

## Benefits
- Merit‑based financial assistance for higher education.  
- Continuation of scholarship support for eligible students through renewal.

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be a college or university student who previously received the PM‑USP scholarship.  
- Renewal applications must be submitted by **31‑10‑2026**.  
- Institute verification must be completed by **15‑11‑2026** and L2 verification by **30‑11‑2026**.  

## Required documents
Not stated in the source.

## How to apply
1. Log in to the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**.  
2. Select the “PM‑USP – Central Sector Scheme Of Scholarship For College And University Students (CSSS)” renewal option.  
3. Complete the renewal form with updated academic and personal details.  
4. Submit the application before 31‑10‑2026.  
5. Ensure your institute completes verification by 15‑11‑2026 and L2 verification by 30‑11‑2026.
