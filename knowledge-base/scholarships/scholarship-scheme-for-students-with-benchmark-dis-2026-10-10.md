## Overview
The scheme provides financial assistance to students with benchmark disabilities at both pre‑matric (classes IX‑X) and post‑matric (classes XI‑XII, diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate) levels. It aims to reduce drop‑out rates, improve participation, and enable dignified livelihoods for disabled learners.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (₹500 per month for day scholars, ₹800 for hostellers at pre‑matric level; rates for post‑matric not detailed in the excerpt).  
- Book grant (₹1,000 per annum for both day scholars and hostellers).  
- Disability allowance (varies by disability type; e.g., visually impaired ₹4,000 per annum, hearing impaired ₹2,000, etc.).  
- Free boarding/lodging allowance at 1/3 of hosteller rates where applicable.  

## Eligibility criteria
- Must be a regular, full‑time student with a benchmark disability.  
- Pre‑matric: Enrolled in classes IX or X in a government or recognized school/board.  
- Post‑matric: Enrolled in classes XI‑XII, diploma, graduate or postgraduate programmes in UGC/AICTE‑recognized institutions (excluding specific training courses listed).  
- 50 % of scholarships each year reserved for girl candidates; unfilled female slots may be allocated to males.  
- Income declaration required (self‑employed parents: certificate from Revenue Officer ≥ Tehsildar; employed parents: consolidated certificate).  
- Aadhaar mandatory as identity document; registration for Unique Disability Identity Card (UDID) recommended.  

## Required documents
1. Income declaration certificate (as per parent/guardian status).  
2. Aadhaar number/EID (and other alternative ID as per portal).  
3. Unique Disability Identity Card (UDID) registration proof (recommended).  
4. School/college certificate confirming enrollment and disability status.  
5. Hostel/approved residence certificate (if applicable).  

## How to apply
1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal: **www.scholarships.gov.in**.  
2. Register and upload all required documents in **.pdf** format.  
3. Ensure the educational institution is also registered on the portal and verifies the applicant’s details.  
4. Submit the application before the prescribed last date.  
5. The State nodal officer reviews applications; the final list is digitally signed on the PFMS portal for DBT disbursal.  
6. For candidates residing in a different state from their permanent residence, the application is considered under the home‑state slot and must be recommended by that state’s Education/Welfare Department.
