## Overview  
The Post‑matric Scholarship supports students with benchmark disabilities pursuing education from Class XI up to Post‑Graduate Degree/Diploma. It covers tuition, maintenance, and other allowances to facilitate higher education.  

## Benefits  
- Scholarship amount (specific quantum not provided in the source).  
- Maintenance allowance, disability allowance, reimbursement of compulsory non‑refundable fees, and book allowance (details not quantified).  

## Eligibility criteria  
- Indian national with a benchmark disability (≥40 % certified).  
- Parents/guardian’s total annual income must not exceed **Rs. 2,50,000**.  
- Same‑parent limit of two eligible children (twins exempt).  
- No other central scholarship/stipend may be received concurrently.  

## Required documents  
1. Certified disability certificate.  
2. Income certificate showing annual income ≤ Rs. 2,50,000.  
3. Academic transcripts and proof of admission to a recognized institution.  
4. Completed application form (not detailed).  

## How to apply  
1. Secure a valid disability certificate.  
2. Obtain income and academic documents.  
3. Complete the prescribed application form (available on DEPwD website).  
4. Submit the application to the designated authority/college.  
5. Follow up for selection and disbursement.
