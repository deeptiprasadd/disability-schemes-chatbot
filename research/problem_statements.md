# Research Paper – Problem Statements

## Project: Disability Schemes Chatbot (RAG-based, Multilingual, India)

---

## Problem Statement 1 — Broad & Impact-Focused
*(Suitable for general AI / social good conferences)*

Despite the existence of numerous government welfare schemes for persons with disabilities
in India, access to accurate and timely information remains severely limited due to
fragmented official sources, complex legal language, and linguistic barriers. This paper
proposes a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based conversational AI system that
automatically aggregates, structures, and delivers scheme information in 13 Indian
languages, bridging the information gap for over 26.8 million persons with disabilities.

---

## Problem Statement 2 — Technically Focused
*(Suitable for NLP / Information Retrieval conferences like ACL, EMNLP)*

Existing question-answering systems for government welfare domains suffer from
hallucination, outdated knowledge, and poor multilingual performance. This work presents
a hybrid retrieval pipeline combining dense semantic search (FAISS), sparse keyword
matching (BM25), and cross-encoder reranking (FlashRank) within a RAG framework,
specifically designed and evaluated for the disability welfare domain in India — a domain
with scarce labeled datasets and high-stakes information needs.

---

## Problem Statement 3 — Accessibility & Human-Centered
*(Suitable for CHI, ASSETS, or HCI-focused conferences)*

Persons with disabilities in India face a compounded challenge: they are the population
most in need of government support, yet least able to navigate the bureaucratic complexity
of welfare scheme eligibility. This paper presents the design, implementation, and
evaluation of an accessible, voice-enabled, multilingual chatbot that leverages RAG to
provide verified, empathetic, and hallucination-resistant information about disability
welfare schemes across central and state governments.

---

## Problem Statement 4 — Short & Concise (One-liner version)
*(Can be used as the opening sentence of the abstract)*

"How can conversational AI be leveraged to make complex government disability welfare
schemes accessible, accurate, and understandable to all citizens, regardless of language
or technical literacy?"
